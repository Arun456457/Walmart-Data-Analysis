Visual Analytics & Strategic Findings

🏪 Total Sales by Store

Store 20 ($301.4M), Store 4 ($299.5M), and Store 14 ($289.0M) lead the enterprise in cumulative volume. In contrast, smaller format locations like Store 33 ($37.2M) and Store 44 ($43.3M) show distinct tiering.

🎄 Peak Holiday Revenue Dates

The single highest grossing weeks were 24-12-2010 ($80.93M) and 23-12-2011 ($77.00M) (Christmas Eve weeks), followed closely by Thanksgiving / Black Friday shopping ($66.59M and $65.82M).

💼 Unemployment Regional Patterns

Stores 12 & 28 show the highest cumulative unemployment values (1,875.66), yet maintained stable weekly baseline revenues, underscoring Walmart's resilience as a discount retailer during economic stress.

🚀 8% Holiday Sales Lift

The Power BI dashboard captures an overall +0.08 (+7.84%) holiday sales lift across all 45 stores, confirming that strategic promotional campaigns reliably expand customer basket size.
